What this view does—and doesn’t—represent

LUNARIS is a concept prototype for NASA Space Apps “Lunar Landing Site Navigator.” Select Load JPL Horizons to request airless apparent Sun and Earth azimuth/elevation at the selected lunar body-fixed coordinates and UTC date.

  • Horizons elevation is relative to the geometric local reference-surface zenith plane, not a LOLA terrain skyline. Terrain masking is not implemented.
  • Solar incidence is a simple sine-of-elevation proxy. It excludes array orientation, shadowing, thermal constraints, degradation, and power-system design.
  • Earth above the geometric horizon does not guarantee a usable radio link. Antenna masks, terrain, link budget, and relay availability are not modeled.
  • Named candidate coordinates are approximate scenario points. Verify coordinates, frame, and datum before operational use.

Sources: JPL Horizons API docs, NASA GSFC south-pole LOLA DEMs, NASA Moon Trek, LROC Quickmap.
